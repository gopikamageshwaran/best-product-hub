Air Fryer Buying Guide

Air fryers are compact convection ovens. Capacity and basket usability usually matter more than the number of preset buttons.

What to check before buying

Capacity

Choose a basket size that matches how many portions you normally cook. Overfilling can reduce browning and airflow.

Counter space

Measure the appliance footprint and leave ventilation space around it according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Temperature control

A useful temperature range and manual time control give you more flexibility than presets alone.

Cleaning

Removable baskets and easy-to-clean surfaces are important because grease can build up quickly.

Cooking expectations

Air frying uses moving hot air, so results differ from deep frying. Small amounts of oil may still improve texture for some foods.

Quick decision tips

  • Choose basket size by household use.
  • Do not overcrowd the basket.
  • Cleaning convenience matters for frequent use.
