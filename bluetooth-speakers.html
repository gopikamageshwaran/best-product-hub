Bluetooth Speaker Buying Guide

Bluetooth speakers range from pocket-size travel models to larger units built for rooms and gatherings. Size should follow where you plan to listen.

What to check before buying

Listening environment

For a desk or bedroom, clarity at moderate volume matters. Outdoor use benefits from stronger output and better water resistance.

Battery life

Treat battery claims as estimates because volume level and features can change real-world runtime.

Durability

If the speaker will travel, look for a sturdy enclosure and an IP rating suited to splashes or dust.

Connections

Check Bluetooth version, multipoint support and whether an AUX input or USB playback matters to you.

Sound profile

Compact speakers often emphasize bass or vocals differently. Independent listening tests are more useful than judging by wattage alone.

Quick decision tips

  • Pick size for your room or travel bag.
  • Outdoor users should prioritize durability.
  • Do not choose by wattage alone.

Final check before checkout
