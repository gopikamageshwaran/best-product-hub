Editorial methodology

How We Choose

Best Product Hub focuses on the questions a shopper should answer before comparing current retailer listings. Our guides are not based on which product generates the highest commission.

1. Define the use case

We first identify who the category is for and what problems it is expected to solve. The right choice for travel, office use or a family kitchen can be very different.

2. Identify meaningful criteria

We focus on measurable or practical factors such as size, compatibility, adjustability, capacity, maintenance, charging standards, materials and warranty considerations.

3. Explain trade-offs

Every category includes compromises. More capacity may add weight; more cushioning may reduce stability; more features may increase complexity. We highlight those trade-offs so shoppers can prioritize.

4. Avoid stale retailer data

We do not publish live Amazon prices, customer star ratings or copied customer reviews. Readers are directed to the current retailer page to verify availability, seller details, specifications and offers.

5. Disclose affiliate links

Amazon.in affiliate links are marked as paid links close to the link itself, and the site also carries the required Associate disclosure.
