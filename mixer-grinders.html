What to check before buying

Motor suitability

Choose power based on what you grind regularly. Tougher ingredients and frequent use may justify a more capable motor.

Jar mix

A useful set usually includes jars sized for small chutney batches, dry grinding and larger wet preparations.

Safety

Overload protection, stable feet and secure lids are practical features for frequent kitchen use.

Cleaning

Simple jar shapes, removable blades where appropriate and fewer hard-to-reach edges make maintenance easier.

Service and warranty

Check brand service coverage in your city and understand which parts are covered by warranty.

Quick decision tips

  • Buy for your actual recipes.
  • Check jar capacity carefully.
  • Local service support can matter more than extra accessories.
