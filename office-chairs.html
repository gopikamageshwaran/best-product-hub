Office Chair Buying Guide

An office chair should fit your body and desk setup. Adjustability is usually more valuable than decorative features when you sit for long periods.

What to check before buying

Seat dimensions

Check seat width, depth and recommended user height so the chair does not feel too shallow or too deep.

Lumbar support

Support should follow the natural curve of your lower back without pushing aggressively into it.

Adjustability

Useful adjustments include seat height, armrests, tilt tension and recline lock. More adjustment can help mixed-use workspaces.

Material

Mesh can improve airflow, while upholstered seats may feel softer. Both depend heavily on construction quality.

Desk fit

Measure your desk height and available floor space, especially if the armrests need to slide under the desk.

Quick decision tips

  • Measure before buying.
  • Prioritize adjustable lumbar and arm support.
  • A footrest may help if your feet do not rest flat.

Final check before checkout

Open the retailer listing and confirm the current model name, specifications, seller, warranty, delivery estimate, return policy, price and any offer conditions. Product listings can change after this guide is published.
