Yoga Mat Buying Guide

The best yoga mat for you depends on how much cushioning you want, how sweaty your practice gets, and whether the mat needs to travel.

What to check before buying

Grip

A stable surface helps reduce slipping. Grip can vary when the mat is dry versus sweaty, so consider your practice style.

Thickness

More cushioning can feel comfortable for knees, while thinner mats usually provide a firmer connection to the floor.

Size

Check length and width against your height and preferred movements, especially for floor exercises beyond yoga.

Material

Materials differ in smell, durability, texture, weight and cleaning needs. Check manufacturer care instructions.

Portability

If you carry the mat to classes, consider rolled size and weight in addition to thickness.

Quick decision tips

  • Choose grip for your practice style.
  • Thicker is not always better for balance.
  • Check care instructions before using cleaners.
